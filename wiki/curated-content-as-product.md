# Curated content as product

Pattern: for small local software — games especially — **the hand-curated data payload is the moat, not the code**. The code of a word game is a weekend; a puzzle bank that's actually *good* is the product.

Factory evidence:

- [groupline](prototype-groupline.md)'s strongest asset is its 15-puzzle bank with **curator-declared overlap-bait** (each puzzle carries words that plausibly read into another group — the property that makes Connections-style puzzles fun). The bait model is validated at the domain boundary and a trickiness bar is test-enforced: curation quality made checkable.
- Backlog ideas whose value is mostly payload: *Hive* (curated word list + solvable letter-set generator), *Waffleworks* (interlocking word grids), *MiniCross* (word+clue bank), *Trilluminate* (hand-authored low-poly scenes as JSON data), *cellforge* (8 levels that are "actually FUN puzzles is real design load").
- **The pattern holds for APPS, not just games.** [sediment](prototype-sediment.md)'s dossier is explicit: "the walk is a weekend, this data is the product." Its OMG caption (*"12 PDFs and a .zip over 4 days — looks like a tax filing"*) is powered entirely by four curated payloads — an extension→**human-facing category** map (chosen for caption phrasing: "photos"/"code project", not filesystem-tidy "Images"), a filename-**token vocabulary** (DCF camera prefixes `IMG_`/`DSC_`/`PXL_` + life-admin stems `1099`/`invoice`/`tax`), the **phrasing/interpretation templates**, and a walk **skip-list** (so a code era reads as "24 code files", not a `node_modules` avalanche). Research that became load-bearing shipped content: the DCF standard (every consumer camera since ~2003 names files `<4-char prefix><4-digit sequence>`) is what makes photo-era token detection *reliable*, not a guess. The same "the corpus is the moat" reasoning is why [cellblame](dev-mechanic-transplant.md)'s hard part is the align-by-key data problem, not the blame fold.
- **Certified content beats hand-trusted content — [foldup](prototype-foldup.md)'s BFS-verified level bank (2026-07-06).** foldup's moat is 8 hand-authored fold puzzles shipped verbatim as a `LEVELS` array, but the payload's *value* is that every level's par is **machine-proven** by the BFS oracle, not hand-guessed — and proving it caught two levels whose authored par was wrong (one target unreachable, one true-par-4-not-2). This is the [solver-verified](solver-verified-puzzle-generation.md) sibling of this page realized as a *game bank*: the curation is the product, and the certification is what makes the curation trustworthy. Same shape as Proofgrid's build-time forcibility check in [novel-puzzle-verbs](novel-puzzle-verbs.md) — a hand-authored bank whose every entry is run through the solver and *rejected* if it fails the guarantee, so the bank and the guarantee are the same artifact.
- **Real numbers beat plausible numbers — [subscription-sleuth](prototype-subscription-sleuth.md)'s price-hike slice (2026-07-06).** The sample's headline demo is the *real* Netflix Standard step **$15.49 → $17.99** (announced Jan 21 2025, +16.1%), not an invented $9.99→$11.99 — a user who paid it recognizes it as true, which is far more convincing than a made-up delta. The dossier curates a **verified 18-row US subscription price-hike table** (Netflix, Spotify $9.99→$10.99→$11.99→$12.99, Hulu, Peacock $7.99→$10.99, Disney+, YouTube Premium, Amazon Prime annual $119→$139, Paramount+, Discovery+ — each with effective date, % step, and source URL) that doubles as (a) the sample-authoring source, (b) a defensibility check for the algorithm's thresholds against real data, and (c) a menu of borderline/cross-cadence cases (Hulu no-ads $17.99→$18.99 at just +5.6%, the annual Amazon step). This is the same class of payload the page already flagged — "real merchant-string formats" — realized for a *different* field of the same app: the content is the price-change history, and researching it *is* building the product.

**Why this matters to the pipeline:** this is the concrete case for the **design-research phase** — research that *becomes shipped content* (a deeper bank, a better word list, real merchant-string formats, genre-defining details) visibly raises product quality in a way code hardening never does. When ideation weighs candidates, "does research compound into the artifact?" is a scoring question.

**Relation to the jaw-dropping bar:** curation alone rarely surprises, but *unexpected corpora* do — a game or tool built on a dataset nobody has assembled (see the dataset-nobody-assembled ideation angle) delivers the OMG through the content itself. Sibling pattern: [solver-verified generation](solver-verified-puzzle-generation.md), where the payload is certified rather than curated.
