# foldup — the fold-the-board-onto-itself puzzle

**Game, v0.** Single-HTML-file, fully-offline grid puzzle whose ONLY verb is FOLD. You crease the whole board along any horizontal or vertical grid line; the smaller half folds onto the larger and every overlapping pair of cells combines by **XOR** (lit+empty→lit, lit+lit→clear). The board strictly **shrinks** each fold; the goal is to fold down to a target pattern in the fewest folds, tracked against a **par**. The wow is the merge/collapse moment — two halves line up, cells cancel or light up, the playfield collapses onto the target. Pure DOM-free `core.js` (`combine`, `fold`, `checkWin`, `solveMinFolds` BFS par oracle, the certified `LEVELS` bank); thin `ui.js` canvas shell with the FLIP animation. Ships v0 pending a human feel-playtest (the fold's physicality can't be confirmed by headless automation — [game feel & juice](game-feel-juice.md)).

**Domain knowledge embodied:**

- **Folding to 1×1 is a parity trivialization — the load-bearing design fact.** Any XOR-fold sequence that collapses a grid to a single cell yields exactly `XOR of all lit cells`, a single bit, *independent of fold order* (GeeksforGeeks, *XOR Folding of a Grid*). So a 1×1 (or "fold it all away to blank") target makes the puzzle a coin-flip — "is the lit count odd?" — with zero spatial reasoning. The fun lives ONLY when the target is **larger than 1×1 with visible structure**, where crease choice and fold order matter. The build ships every target ≥ 2×1 and a validator that rejects a 1×1 target. This is the fold-mechanic's specific instance of the general "[reject the vacuous form](solver-verified-puzzle-generation.md)" discipline.
- **The certified level bank is the moat — 8 levels, all BFS-verified, shipped verbatim as DATA.** The strongest [curated content as product](curated-content-as-product.md) instance where the payload is *certified* rather than hand-trusted: BFS `solveMinFolds` proved each level's tagged par is the true minimum AND caught **two** hand-authored levels with wrong par (one target unreachable by the intended fold, one whose true minimum was 4 not 2). The bank is pre-sorted as the difficulty ramp (1-fold tutorials → 2-fold → 3-fold), each level introducing one idea (lit-onto-empty, cancellation, second axis, overhang).
- **The fold is a half-plane mirror; fold the SMALLER side onto the LARGER.** `fold(grid, axis, index)` reflects one half across the crease (`dest = 2*index-1-j`), combines overlapping cells by XOR, and carries **overhang** cells (no incoming mirror on an asymmetric crease) over unchanged. Folding smaller-onto-larger shrinks the board by exactly the folded side and never silently loses information. Ties fold right/bottom onto left/top — a fixed, legible rule the flip animation must honor (the sweeping flap is always the smaller side).
- **The BFS fold-space is tiny because the board strictly shrinks.** Branching is `~2*(N-1)` creases and drops as the board contracts; the target size lower-bounds it, so BFS-by-fold-depth terminates fast (certified all 8 levels in <1s). Visited states must be keyed by **content AND dimensions** so a pattern match at the wrong size is never a false win. This is why the general #P-hardness of map-folding ([arXiv 2410.07666](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.07666)) doesn't bite: constraining folds to single grid-line creases keeps the tree shallow and narrow — [solver-verified generation](solver-verified-puzzle-generation.md) made tractable by a constrained move set.

**Connections:** fold-the-playfield-onto-itself is the unclaimed verb in [novel puzzle verbs](novel-puzzle-verbs.md) (its top game-round pick, `wow 8 / feasible 9`); the merge/collapse FLIP moment is the [game feel & juice](game-feel-juice.md) lever (teleporting to the resolved state would throw away the wow — same lesson chainfall learned); the certified par bank is [solver-verified generation](solver-verified-puzzle-generation.md) + [curated content as product](curated-content-as-product.md); the architecture is [pure core, thin shell](pure-core-thin-shell.md). Unlike [chainfall](prototype-chainfall.md) / [groupline](prototype-groupline.md), foldup fills the gap NOT as an [ad-ruined revival](ad-ruined-casual-games.md) but as a genuinely *new* verb — the higher-wow route that page names.

**Open direction (toward v1):** human feel-playtest to confirm the fold reads as physical; hide-par-until-first-solve toggle (Unflip's crowd asked for this — [daily-puzzle rituals](daily-puzzle-rituals.md)); a solver-verified generator (credible v1 vein now that `solveMinFolds` exists); combine rules that *create* lit cells to fight endgame trivialization on deep folds.
